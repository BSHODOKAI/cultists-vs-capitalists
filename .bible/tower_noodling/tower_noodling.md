Proposal: Substation Effigy (substation_effigy)
1. How Does It Fit the Theme?
Visual & Lore Concept (Lovecraftian Street Cult + Salvaged Urban Junk):
The cultists have dragged a stolen municipal pole-pig transformer and a tangle of stripped high-voltage copper feeder cables onto a concrete hex plinth, lash-welding them into a horned wicker-and-rebar idol crowned with cracked porcelain insulators and ritual lightning rods.
Instead of a clean sci-fi Tesla tower, it looks like an illegal street-grid tap crossed with an occult storm totem—humming with stolen city megawatts and eldritch ozone.
Branch Flavors:
Revealed Branch (Offerings + Corruption): The cult uses the high-voltage arcs as a carrier wave for void-storm entities (Astral Conduction $\rightarrow$ Voice in the Static), turning lightning into living, storm-calling liturgy that arcs further and marks/grounds airborne targets.
Rigged Branch (Scrap + Jank): The cult hot-wires the effigy straight into the district's main breaker with jury-rigged capacitor banks (Substation Tap $\rightarrow$ Breaker Cascade), cranking up arc count, anti-air voltage, and micro-EMP overload bursts at the risk of blowing its own fuses (Jank).
